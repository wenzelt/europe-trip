The road, stop by stop

Munich to Tarifa is a long way, so we take it in short stages: a night in Bern, a night in Lyon, then down the Mediterranean coast to Andalusia.

Tarifa for a week in March, Cádiz over Easter, the Algarve in April, and home to Munich on 25 April.

Alps and France

8 – 12 Feb

Bern, Lyon, Montpellier, Girona.

Spanish coast

12 Feb – 20 Mar

Peñíscola, Alicante, Almería, Nerja, Sevilla.

Tarifa

20 – 27 Mar

The kite week, at the southern tip of Spain.

Cádiz

27 Mar – 3 Apr

Cádiz and El Puerto de Santa María, over Easter.

Algarve

3 – 11 Apr

Tavira and the eastern Algarve, in Portugal.

The way home

11 – 25 Apr

Nerja, Cartagena, Valencia, Girona, Montpellier, Lyon, Bern, Munich.
